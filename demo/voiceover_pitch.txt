## S3 PRODUCT [0:50-1:40]

through a five-line interface. It runs twelve tests — recall, abstention on unanswerables,
adversarial traps — and issues a score plus a SHA-256 fingerprint anyone can recompute.
The engine stays black-box by design. We publish fingerprints, never contents.
