## Descrizione
Pitch for our Colosseum Crypto World's Fair submission (Solana track).

Keycard certifies who an agent is. MemPolygraph certifies whether its memory tells
the truth: black-box tests (recall, abstention, adversarial) produce a signed score
plus a SHA-256 fingerprint anyone can recompute. Engine stays black-box by design.

Same method, live registry: one agent went 71.87 to 90.0 Elder, with falsifiable
predictions moving the score.

Repo: https://github.com/marcot3ssar1/mempolygraph
